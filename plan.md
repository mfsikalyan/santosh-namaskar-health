# Namaskar Home Health Care Service — Antigravity Website Plan

## 1. Project Overview

Build a modern, trustworthy, responsive website for **NAMASKAR HOME HEALTH CARE SERVICE**, a professional home nursing and healthcare service.

The website should feel:

- Professional
- Safe
- Caring
- Hygienic
- Trustworthy
- Premium but approachable
- Easy for elderly patients and family members to use
- Highly optimized for mobile because many visitors will come from phone/WhatsApp

Use the provided Namaskar branding/logo images as the primary visual reference.

### Existing brand information

**Business Name:** NAMASKAR HOME HEALTH CARE SERVICE

**Primary phone / WhatsApp:** +91 9827772983

**Availability:** 24x7 Home Service / Available 24 Hours - 7 Days a Week

**Existing service messaging:**
- Professional
- Hygienic
- Safe Patient Care

### Existing services shown in the reference artwork

- Wound Dressing & Post-Surgery Care
- Injection, Saline & IV Drip Setup
- Catheter Care & Daily Vitals Check

# 2. Primary Design Direction

Create a **premium healthcare website rather than a generic hospital template**.

The visual identity should be based on the existing logo and promotional artwork.

### Brand colors

Use the existing palette as the foundation:

- Deep Navy / Healthcare Blue: `#063B5A` or a visually similar deep navy
- Royal/Dark Blue: `#063A78`
- Teal Green: `#079B84`
- Gold/Yellow: `#E5B52B`
- White: `#FFFFFF`
- Soft Healthcare Gray: `#F3F6F8`
- Dark Text: `#183247`

Use gold as an accent rather than the dominant color.

Use teal for health/safety/action elements.

Use navy for trust, headings, navigation, and major sections.

The website should have generous whitespace and a clean medical aesthetic.

# 3. Target Audience

Design primarily for:

1. Adult children looking for home nursing care for parents
2. Elderly patients
3. Post-surgery patients and their families
4. Patients requiring regular nursing support
5. Families needing urgent home nursing visits
6. People searching for home healthcare services through Google
7. Mobile users coming directly from WhatsApp

The website should make the answer to these questions immediately obvious:

- What services do you provide?
- Can you come to my home?
- Are you available 24/7?
- How do I contact you?
- Can I call or WhatsApp?
- What type of nursing care can I request?

# 33. Content Rules

Do not fabricate business information.

If information is missing, create a visually appropriate placeholder that can easily be edited later.

Especially do not invent:

- Physical address
- Number of nurses
- Number of patients
- Years of experience
- Medical licenses
- Government registrations
- Hospital partnerships
- Doctor affiliations
- Testimonials
- Awards
- Pricing
- Service areas
- Google ratings

Create configuration variables/constants for editable business information.

Example:

```ts
const BUSINESS = {
  name: "Namaskar Home Health Care Service",
  phone: "+91 9827772983",
  availability: "24 Hours • 7 Days a Week",
  whatsappMessage:
    "Hello Namaskar Home Health Care Service, I would like to know more about your home nursing services."
};
```

This should make the website easy to customize for future clients.
